How the planner works

Browse the catalog on the left, add pieces to the floor plan, and arrange them to scale. Everything saves automatically in your browser.

DragMove a piece around the floor plan
ClickSelect a piece and show its details
RRotate the selected piece 90°
DDuplicate the selected piece
BackspaceRemove the selected piece
EscDeselect / close dialogs
Ctrl+ZUndo the last change
